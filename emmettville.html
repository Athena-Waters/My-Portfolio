Emmettville

Community theater poster design

Emmettville is a community theater production written by Amy O'Leary, presented in association with the Gem County Recreation District. The poster needed to sell the show's personality, full of hometown warmth, small-town charm, and a sense of fun, while still reading clearly as an event announcement.

The design centers on an illustrated water tower standing tall above a field of wild grass, a nod to the actual Gem County landmark. Warm cream tones and earthy reds keep the piece grounded, while the classic serif headline gives it a storybook feel that matches the show's tone.

The poster includes all the essentials like dates, times, venue, and ticket pricing, all arranged so the eye moves naturally from the show title down to the details. It was built to work as both a large-format print piece and a scaled-down flyer.
