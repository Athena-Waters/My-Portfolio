Nova's Bubble Baths

Brand identity & business card design

Nova's Bubble Baths is a freelance mobile dog bathing service built around a simple promise: a squeaky-clean pup without the hassle of wet hair, soaked floors, and bustling salons. The brand needed to feel friendly, trustworthy, and a little bit playful because the audience is dog owners who want the best for their pets, but also appreciate a service that comes to them.

The identity centers on a custom illustrated portrait of Nova, the dog who inspired the business, a happy, tongue-out pup framed inside a circular badge with floating bubbles. The soft purple palette keeps things warm and approachable, while clean sans-serif typography balances the playfulness with professionalism.

The business card carries the brand into the real world. The front leads with the logo, while the back keeps it simple with just a name, title, a short tagline, and contact details which lets a single card do the job of introducing the business and starting a conversation.

Software Used

  • Ibis Paint X
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Adobe Photoshop
