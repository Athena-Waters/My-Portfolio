Midnight Jazz

Event flyer design

Midnight Jazz is an evening performance at the Blue Note Lounge, and the flyer was designed to feel like the room it's promoting — intimate, low-lit, and full of atmosphere. The piece leans into a duotone palette of greys and warm shadows, letting the mood carry more weight than the color.

The centerpiece is a silhouette of a saxophone player, rendered as a subtle, film-grain texture rather than a crisp illustration. This gives the piece a vintage, almost cinematic quality that matches the tone of a late-night jazz set.

Typography is kept simple and classic with bold serif for the show name and sans-serif for the details. The layout was designed to work at multiple scales, from small flyers to larger lobby prints, without losing any of its mood.
