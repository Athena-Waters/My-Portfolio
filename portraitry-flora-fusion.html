Portraitry & Flora Fusion

Editorial spread & magazine layout

Portraitry & Flora Fusion is a conceptual editorial spread that pairs portrait photography with floral art, exploring how the two can fuse together to create something a little surreal. The piece was designed as a magazine feature with a clean, modern layout that lets the imagery take center stage.

The layout leans on strong grid structure and generous white space, letting each image and headline breathe. Serif typography anchors the editorial voice, while the floral elements create visual rhythm from page to page. Every spread was composed so the eye moves naturally from image to text and back again.

The challenge was balance: making the imagery feel cohesive across spreads, keeping the typography legible against busy backgrounds, and giving the whole piece a real editorial feel.
