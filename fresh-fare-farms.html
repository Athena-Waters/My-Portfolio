Fresh Fare Farms

Multi-channel advertising campaign

Fresh Fare Farms is a local farm-to-table service that delivers fresh produce boxes to families in its community. The campaign was built around a simple but powerful mission: keep your community fresh. That idea drives every piece, from the magazine ad to the social media series, tying together seasonal food, local farms, and the bigger story of food access.

The design system uses a warm, earthy palette of greens, oranges, and cream tones, with a hand-lettered logo and friendly sans-serif typography. Photography of real produce and real people doing real work anchors everything, keeping the campaign grounded in the community it serves rather than feeling like polished stock imagery.

The magazine ad pairs a full-page image with a clean layout of supporting copy, the brand call-to-action, and a link to sign up. The social media series carries the same visual language into a series of posts, each one focused on a different angle of the mission, so together they tell the full story.
