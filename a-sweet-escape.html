A Sweet Escape

Book cover & spine design

A Sweet Escape is a modern romance novel cover and spine design that built around the idea that love stories should feel warm, inviting, and a little bit cinematic. The novel is a contemporary romance set against a cozy, soft-lit world, and the cover needed to signal that mood instantly. It's the kind of book you'd pick up on a rainy afternoon and finish in one sitting.

The cover pairs rich, atmospheric photography with a hand-lettered script title, letting the imagery set the tone while the typography adds personality and warmth. The spine carries the design forward with a clean vertical layout, so the book reads beautifully whether it's sitting on a shelf, a nightstand, or in someone's hands.

The goal was to make the book feel tactile and inviting before anyone opens it — the kind of cover you'd want to leave out on display, not tuck away. Warm tones, soft contrast, and generous spacing all work together to create that feeling.
