Sister Squad Series

Custom apparel for four sisters

The Sister Squad Series is a set of four custom shirts designed for four sisters with each one built around her personality. The Dramatic One, The Sassy One, The Extra One, and The Quiet One each got her own design, pairing a custom saying with artwork of her favorite animal, provided by the client.

The challenge with a series like this is making four designs feel like they belong together without making them feel identical. Each shirt needed to stand on its own and match the wearer, while the whole set needed a shared visual language with the same illustration style, typographic feel, and personality-forward energy.

The original animal art was provided by the client and then refined and composed with custom typography for print. One of the four designs was produced on a hoodie at the client's request, while the rest were printed on tees.

Software Used

  • Ibis Paint X
  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Adobe Illustrator
