Hey Angel

Custom hoodie design

Hey Angel is a custom hoodie design inspired by the Harry Styles song of the same name. It is a piece made for a client who wanted to wear something that felt personal to a song that means a lot to her. The design pairs illustrated angel wings with a floating halo and hand-drawn "Hey Angel" typography to create a piece that feels like a wearable keepsake.

Part of the fun of a piece like this is that it's a small, personal tribute. Everything from the softness of the wing details to the placement of the halo was chosen to make the design feel playful and reverent at the same time.

Software Used

  • Ibis Paint X
  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Adobe Illustrator
