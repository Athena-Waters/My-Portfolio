Harvest & Hops

Event marketing campaign

Harvest & Hops is a craft beer and autumn feast event hosted at Riverfront Park - an afternoon of live music, local food trucks, and 10+ regional breweries. The campaign needed to sell the whole experience, not just the beer, but also the mood, the community, and the promise of a great Saturday.

The design system centers on a warm cream base with bold orange accents and classic serif typography which is a nod to seasonal harvest signage and traditional event posters. Every asset in the campaign uses the same visual language which means that the brochure, promotional posters, and social media graphics all feel like they belong to the same event.

The trifold brochure leads with a full-bleed hero image of a craft beer, then unfolds into event details, ticket pricing, and a lineup of food and music. The social posts are designed to work as standalone announcements and as a series with each one focusing on a different draw of the event, so together they build up the full picture for anyone scrolling through.
