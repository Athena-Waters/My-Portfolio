Piddle Paddle Tours

Tourism brochure design

Piddle Paddle Tours is a Pacific Northwest adventure company offering whale watching, lighthouse visits, sea kayaking, and coastal excursions. The brochure needed to do a lot of work at once by inviting visitors in and presenting multiple tour options clearly while still feeling like it belonged to the region it was selling.

The design leads with a bold, hand-lettered logo and a friendly call-to-action on the cover. Inside, each tour gets its own section with custom photography, descriptive copy, and a clear visual hierarchy so readers can scan and find the experience they're looking for without wading through walls of text.

The palette is pulled straight from the coast with deep blues, warm sand tones, and clean whites to give the photography room to breathe. The trifold format keeps everything tactile and easy to hold, which matters for a piece that's meant to be picked up and taken home.
