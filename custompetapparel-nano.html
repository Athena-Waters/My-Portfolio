Custom Pet Apparel — Nano

Custom illustrated apparel

Nano is a goofy, lovable St. Bernard with a personality almost as big as he is. This custom hoodie features a hand-drawn portrait of him that captures his floppy ears, droopy jowls, and his tongue-out grin which is paired with bold, custom typography .

This piece is the first in an ongoing series of pet portrait apparel. The goal was to translate a beloved pet into a wearable design that feels personal, playful, and like something you'd actually want to wear out in public, not just around the house.

The artwork was illustrated digitally in Ibis Paint X, then brought into Photoshop and Illustrator to refine for print. Line weight and contrast were carefully tuned to hold up across different apparel formats from hoodies to tees to smaller merch pieces.

Software Used

  • Ibis Paint X
  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Adobe Illustrator
