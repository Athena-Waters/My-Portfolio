Poetry Night

Event flyer design

Poetry Night is an evening of spoken word and open mic, hosted at a local café. The flyer was designed to feel like the event itself with no loud graphics or busy layouts; just good typography doing the work.

The layout leads with the event name in a classic serif, supported by clean sans-serif details for the featured readers, date, and venue. Textured paper and subtle layering give the piece a printed, tactile feel.

The challenge was keeping the hierarchy clear while letting the piece breathe. Every element has a job, and nothing fights for attention. It reads as elegant without being precious.
